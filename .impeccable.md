## Design Context

### Users
SMB Real Estate Brokers L.L.C. is a Dubai brokerage (est. 2021, Business Bay) serving off-plan and secondary buyers: individual/first-time buyers, families, property investors, property owners, and commercial businesses. Visitors are typically evaluating a high-consideration, high-value purchase — they arrive to compare developers/projects, check pricing and payment plans, and reach a consultant (call, WhatsApp, or enquiry form). A meaningful share of business is repeat clients/referrals, so the site also has to read as credible to people who already trust the brand.

### Brand Personality
Client brief (docs/design-brief.md) states the identity should read as: Professionalism, Trust, Luxury, Confidence, Simplicity, Transparency, Modern Business, Premium Service.
Explicitly avoid: overly playful design, heavy gradients, excessive animation, visual clutter.
Primary palette: Navy #0a2342 (brand/trust) + Golden Yellow #f9ba3f (accent/CTA). Manrope is the specified typeface. Photography should be real (not renders), bright/natural light, luxury architecture/interiors — generic stock is to be avoided.

### Aesthetic Direction
Theme: light (white/warm-white surfaces, navy reserved for header/footer/dark accent sections, gold as a rare CTA/highlight color) — this is what's implemented and matches "premium daytime real estate" browsing.
No anti-references or specific competitor sites have been given yet — flagged as an open question below.
Current implementation (assets/css/main.css, home.css, developers.css) is faithful to the brief's literal tokens (colors, 12–24px radius, soft shadows, Manrope) but has flattened into one repeating structural pattern across every page: dark hero with gradient scrim + floating white lead-gen card, then a `.section-head` (eyebrow + h2) followed by a grid of identical white rounded cards (icon-in-a-box + heading + short copy) — this shape is reused near-verbatim for facts, stat-cards, amenity-cards, place-cards, project-cards, and dev-cards, and the project template (templates/project-template.php) is deliberately reused byte-for-byte across every project page. This repetition is the direct cause of the "just a template" feedback.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the token-level brand contract (navy/gold, Manrope, WCAG 2.2 AA, no heavy gradients/excessive motion) — creativity should come from composition and hierarchy, not new colors or a personality swap.
2. Break the "icon-in-rounded-box card grid" as the default answer to every content block; each section type (facts, amenities, location, gallery) should get its own distinct structural treatment instead of the same card shape restyled.
3. Vary section rhythm and layout on purpose (asymmetry, differing widths/backgrounds, one true full-bleed/editorial moment per page) rather than uniform `.section` + `.container` + centered grid on every block.
4. Typography and photography are the brand's actual differentiators (real luxury photography, confident type scale) — lean on them harder before reaching for decorative UI chrome.
5. Preserve the shared-partial architecture (project-template.php, developer-card.php, enquiry-form.php) per [[wordpress_conversion_plan]] and [[shared_component_preference]] — fix the visual monotony inside the shared system, don't fork per-project templates.
