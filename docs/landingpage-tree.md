## Sticky Call-to-Action

To improve lead generation, a lightweight sticky call-to-action should be used instead of displaying the contact form throughout the page.

Desktop

- Display a small floating card at the bottom-right corner after the Hero Section.
- Keep comfortable spacing from the viewport edges.
- The card should contain:
  - Starting From
  - AED X
  - Fill The Form

Mobile

- Display the same call-to-action as a floating snackbar-style card.
- Keep horizontal and bottom margins.
- Do not stretch edge-to-edge.

Interaction

- Clicking the card should smoothly scroll to the contact form located in the Final Call-to-Action section.
- Do not display a second form or open a modal.
- Hide the floating card when the Final Call-to-Action section becomes visible.
