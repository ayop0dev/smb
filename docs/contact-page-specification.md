# Contact Page Specification

## Objective

Create a premium corporate Contact page for **SMB Real Estate Brokers L.LC**.

The page must make it easy for visitors to contact the company, send an enquiry, call, use WhatsApp, or visit the office.

The design must feel elegant, clear, trustworthy, and consistent with the homepage, About page, and project landing page.

Do not create an experimental or over-designed layout.

---

## Visual Direction

The page should feel:

- Premium
- Professional
- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Spacious
- Modern
- Refined
- Easy to use

Avoid:

- Heavy animation
- Complex interactions
- Excessive cards
- Decorative graphics
- Crowded layouts
- Long text blocks
- Generic form-template styling

Use the SMB brand colors:

- Navy Blue: `#0a2342`
- Golden Yellow: `#f9ba3f`

Gold must be used sparingly as an accent.

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Inner Page Hero

Purpose:

Introduce the page and guide the visitor toward contact.

Content:

- Eyebrow: `Contact SMB`
- Main heading
- Short supporting paragraph
- Optional breadcrumb
- Supporting background or image

Suggested heading direction:

`Let’s Discuss Your Property Goals`

Suggested supporting direction:

Contact SMB Real Estate Brokers for property enquiries, buying, selling, managing, or investment support in Dubai.

The hero must be shorter than the homepage hero.

---

## 2. Contact Overview

Purpose:

Present the main contact methods clearly.

Display three contact options:

- Call
- WhatsApp
- Email

Each item must include:

- Small icon
- Clear label
- Contact value
- Direct action link

Use clean premium blocks.

Do not use oversized icons or heavy card styling.

---

## 3. Contact Form

Purpose:

Collect enquiries.

Required fields:

- Full Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Enquiry Type
- Message
- Submit Button

Suggested enquiry types:

- Buying Property
- Selling Property
- Property Investment
- Property Management
- General Enquiry

Form requirements:

- Clear labels
- Accessible inputs
- Visible focus states
- Inline validation
- Useful error messages
- Success message
- Mobile-friendly fields
- Consistent field heights and spacing

Do not add unnecessary fields.

The form must feel trustworthy and simple.

---

## 4. Office Information

Purpose:

Show the company’s physical location and business details.

Include:

- Office address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Working hours

Use the available company details:

- Phone: `+971 50 421 7299`
- Email: `info@smbdubai.net`
- Address: `3002 Westburry Tower Office, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE`

Working hours may use temporary content if the final schedule is not confirmed.

Mark temporary information clearly.

---

## 5. Map

Purpose:

Help visitors locate the office.

Include:

- Embedded map or map placeholder
- Office location marker
- Direct link to open the location in Google Maps

The map must not dominate the page.

Keep the layout clean and proportionate.

If the final map embed is unavailable, use a clearly marked placeholder.

---

## 6. Visit or Speak With Us

Purpose:

Add a personal contact prompt before the final CTA.

Content direction:

Invite visitors to speak with a property consultant or arrange an office visit.

Structure:

- Short heading
- Short paragraph
- Call button
- WhatsApp button

Keep this section concise.

---

## 7. Final CTA

Purpose:

Provide a strong closing action.

Include:

- Strong headline
- Short supporting text
- Primary enquiry button
- WhatsApp button

Suggested heading direction:

`Ready to Make Your Next Property Move?`

---

## 8. Footer

Reuse the same corporate footer system used across the website.

Include:

- Company information
- Quick links
- Contact information
- Social links
- Copyright

# Layout Notes

## Desktop

Use a two-column layout for the main contact section:

- Left: contact form
- Right: office information and contact details

The map should appear below as a wide section.

## Tablet

Keep the form and office information balanced.

Stack only when needed.

## Mobile

Stack all content vertically.

Keep actions full-width where appropriate.

Ensure contact buttons remain easy to tap.

---

# General Rules

- Keep the page consistent with the homepage and About page.
- Reuse the same header, footer, typography, buttons, forms, spacing, and design tokens.
- Maintain generous spacing.
- Keep the content concise.
- Use subtle background changes between sections.
- Avoid excessive borders and shadows.
- Use restrained hover and reveal effects.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts are complete.
- Do not add autoplay carousels.
- Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies.
- Do not invent office branches, phone numbers, staff names, or business claims.
- Do not use unverified working hours as final content.
- Keep all contact actions functional.
- Preserve accessibility.

The final page must make it immediately clear:

- How to call SMB
- How to contact SMB through WhatsApp
- How to send an enquiry
- Where the office is located
- When and how the company will respond
