# Project Summary

DAMAC Islands 2 is the second phase of the DAMAC Islands master community located in Dubailand, Dubai.

The project introduces an island-inspired residential lifestyle centered around crystal lagoons, landscaped parks, waterfront living, and resort-style amenities. The master community is inspired by some of the world's most recognizable tropical island destinations and is designed around nature, wellness, recreation, and luxury family living.

The development targets both end users seeking luxury suburban living and investors looking for long-term capital appreciation within Dubai's off-plan market.

# Master Plan

## Overview

DAMAC Islands 2 is planned as a large-scale waterfront community built around interconnected lagoons, landscaped public spaces, and island-inspired residential districts.

## Island Concept

The community draws inspiration from eight tropical destinations:

- Bahamas
- Tahiti
- Bermuda
- Barbados
- Cuba
- Maui
- Mauritius
- Antigua

Each district features its own landscape identity while sharing the same overall waterfront lifestyle concept.

## Districts

Current launches include selected clusters such as:

- Mauritius
- Antigua

Additional island districts are planned for future releases.

## Landscape

The community emphasizes:

- Extensive green areas
- Walking trails
- Tropical planting
- Open parks
- Waterfront promenades

## Water Features

- Crystal lagoons
- Artificial beaches
- Waterfront views
- Water recreation areas

## Community Layout

The masterplan combines residential neighborhoods with central recreational areas, retail services, parks, sports facilities, and community destinations connected through landscaped streets and pedestrian-friendly public spaces.
