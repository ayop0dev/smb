# Homepage Specification

## Objective

Design a premium corporate homepage for **SMB Real Estate Brokers L.LC**.

The homepage should represent the company, build trust, and direct visitors toward projects and enquiries.

The visual quality should feel premium, elegant, and modern while remaining realistic and economical to implement.

Do not attempt to create an experimental or award-style website.

The design should feel similar in quality and structure to leading Dubai real estate companies.

---

# Visual Style

The homepage should feel:

- Premium
- Elegant
- Spacious
- Editorial
- Modern
- Clean
- Professional
- Luxury without being excessive

Avoid:

- Heavy animations
- Complex interactions
- Over-designed layouts
- Trendy visual effects
- Large decorative graphics
- Unnecessary complexity

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Hero

Purpose:

Create a strong first impression.

Content:

- Large lifestyle background image
- Main headline
- Short supporting paragraph
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Small project search / browse entry point

The hero should sell Dubai lifestyle first, not a specific project.

---

## 2. Featured Projects

Display a curated selection of premium projects.

Structure:

- Section title
- Short description
- Grid of featured projects

Each project card should include:

- Cover image
- Project name
- Location
- Developer
- Starting price
- CTA

Maximum:

4–6 projects.

---

## 3. Why SMB

Purpose:

Build trust.

Structure:

Small premium feature blocks.

Examples:

- Local Market Expertise
- Verified Projects
- Transparent Guidance
- End-to-End Support

Avoid oversized icons.

Focus on typography and spacing.

---

## 4. Property Categories

Allow visitors to browse properties by type.

Examples:

- Apartments
- Villas
- Townhouses
- Waterfront
- Commercial
- Investment

Display as clean premium cards.

---

## 5. Featured Communities

Highlight Dubai communities instead of individual projects.

Examples:

- Downtown Dubai
- Dubai Marina
- Business Bay
- Dubai Hills
- Palm Jumeirah
- Dubai Creek Harbour

Each card should encourage exploration.

---

## 6. Buying Process

Simple four-step timeline.

Examples:

1. Discover
2. Consult
3. Reserve
4. Own

Minimal and elegant.

---

## 7. Testimonials

Display a small number of testimonials.

Maximum:

3 testimonials.

No autoplay carousel.

---

## 8. Latest Insights

Latest articles from the blog.

Display:

3 articles.

Magazine-inspired layout.

---

## 9. Final CTA

Large closing section.

Purpose:

Encourage enquiries.

Include:

- Strong headline
- Short paragraph
- Call button
- WhatsApp button

---

## 10. Footer

Corporate footer.

Include:

- Company information
- Quick links
- Contact information
- Social links
- Copyright

# General Rules

Maintain generous spacing.

Keep typography calm and elegant.

Use a restrained color palette.

Allow sections to breathe.

Reduce visual noise.

Use large imagery.

Keep cards simple.

Avoid excessive borders.

Use subtle shadows.

Keep animations minimal.

Focus on consistency.

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- Trust
- Luxury
- Professionalism
- Dubai Lifestyle
- Investment Opportunity

without feeling like a template or an experimental concept.
