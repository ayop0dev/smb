# Services Page Specification

## Objective

Create a premium corporate Services page for **SMB Real Estate Brokers L.LC**.

The page must clearly explain SMB's real estate services, highlight its off-plan advisory expertise, and guide visitors toward contacting the company.

The design must remain consistent with the Homepage, About page, Contact page, and Project Landing Pages.

Do not create an experimental or over-designed layout.

---

## Visual Direction

The page should feel:

- Premium
- Professional
- Modern
- Spacious
- Trustworthy
- Elegant
- Clear

Avoid:

- Heavy animations
- Complex interactions
- Generic feature grids
- Large decorative graphics
- Long paragraphs
- Excessive cards
- Over-designed layouts

Use the SMB brand colors:

- Navy Blue: `#0a2342`
- Golden Yellow: `#f9ba3f`

Use gold sparingly as an accent.

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Inner Page Hero

Purpose:

Introduce SMB's services and establish a premium first impression.

Content:

- Eyebrow: `Our Services`
- Main heading
- Short supporting paragraph
- Optional breadcrumb
- Supporting lifestyle image

Suggested heading direction:

`Real Estate Services Built Around Your Goals`

Suggested supporting direction:

SMB supports buyers, sellers, landlords, investors, and businesses with clear guidance and professional real estate services across Dubai.

The hero must be shorter than the homepage hero.

---

## 2. Services Introduction

Purpose:

Provide a concise overview of the company's service approach.

Content should explain that SMB supports clients throughout the full real estate journey, from the first consultation to the completion of the transaction.

Focus on:

- Personalized guidance
- Dubai market knowledge
- Clear communication
- Professional representation
- Support across residential and commercial properties

Keep the copy concise and easy to scan.

---

## 3. Core Services

Display the main services in a premium grid.

Required services:

### Off-Plan Property Advisory

Help clients evaluate and secure suitable off-plan opportunities based on budget, goals, payment plans, and long-term value.

### Residential Property Sales

Support buyers and sellers with property selection, pricing, negotiation, and transaction coordination.

### Commercial Real Estate

Assist businesses and investors with suitable offices, retail spaces, and other commercial property opportunities.

### Property Investment Advisory

Guide investors through market opportunities, potential returns, property comparison, and long-term investment planning.

### Property Leasing

Support landlords and tenants with property marketing, tenant search, rental negotiation, and leasing procedures.

### Property Management

Help property owners manage their assets, tenants, communication, and day-to-day property requirements.

Each service item must include:

- Small icon or number
- Service title
- Short description
- CTA or text link

Keep the cards light and refined.

Do not make the section look like a software features grid.

---

## 4. Off-Plan Property Advisory

Purpose:

Give additional emphasis to SMB's off-plan expertise without turning the section into a developer showcase.

Structure:

- Two-column layout
- Concise content on one side
- Supporting image on the other side

Content should explain that SMB helps clients identify, compare, and evaluate off-plan opportunities from established developers in Dubai.

Focus on:

- Understanding investment goals
- Comparing suitable projects
- Reviewing payment plans
- Evaluating location and community potential
- Understanding handover expectations
- Supporting reservation and transaction steps

Do not:

- list developer names
- display developer logos
- showcase individual projects
- repeat the Projects or Developers page content

This section must explain the advisory service, not promote specific developers.

---

## 5. Why Choose SMB

Purpose:

Explain the practical value of working with the company.

Suggested points:

- Local Dubai market knowledge
- Honest and transparent guidance
- Personalized property recommendations
- Support from enquiry to completion
- Responsive communication
- Long-term client relationships

Use a clean split layout, numbered list, or structured text blocks.

Do not repeat the exact descriptions from the Core Services section.

---

## 6. Our Process

Display a simple four-step process.

### 1. Consultation

Understand the client's goals, budget, timeline, and property requirements.

### 2. Shortlisting

Identify and compare suitable property opportunities.

### 3. Negotiation and Coordination

Support pricing, documentation, communication, and transaction details.

### 4. Completion and Support

Guide the client through completion and provide continued support when needed.

Use a horizontal process on desktop and stacked steps on mobile.

Keep the design minimal.

---

## 7. Who We Help

Purpose:

Clarify the main customer groups.

Include:

- Home Buyers
- Property Sellers
- Landlords
- Real Estate Investors
- Businesses Seeking Commercial Property

Use concise content blocks.

Do not add unnecessary personas or fictional examples.

---

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

Display 5–6 common questions related to the services.

Suggested questions:

- How does SMB help buyers find the right property?
- Can SMB assist with off-plan investments?
- Do you support property sellers?
- Can you help with residential and commercial properties?
- What does property management include?
- How can I start a consultation?

Use a clean accordion.

Keep answers concise.

---

## 9. Final CTA

Purpose:

Encourage visitors to discuss their property requirements.

Include:

- Strong headline
- Short supporting text
- Primary contact button
- WhatsApp button

Suggested heading direction:

`Let’s Find the Right Property Solution for You`

---

## 10. Footer

Reuse the same corporate footer system used across the website.

Include:

- Company information
- Quick links
- Contact information
- Social links
- Copyright

# General Rules

- Keep the page consistent with the Homepage, About page, and Contact page.
- Reuse the same header, footer, typography, buttons, cards, spacing, and design tokens.
- Maintain generous spacing.
- Keep paragraphs narrow and readable.
- Use subtle background changes between sections.
- Keep cards simple.
- Avoid excessive borders and shadows.
- Use subtle hover and reveal effects.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure complete desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Do not add pricing tables.
- Do not add developer logos.
- Do not list specific developers.
- Do not showcase projects on this page.
- Do not invent services outside SMB's real estate scope.
- Do not add unverifiable statistics, awards, or achievements.
- Do not add autoplay carousels.
- Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies.

The final page must clearly communicate:

- What services SMB provides
- How SMB supports clients
- SMB's expertise in off-plan advisory
- Who the services are for
- How the process works
- How to contact the company
