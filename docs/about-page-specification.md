# About Page Specification

## Objective

Create a premium corporate About page for **SMB Real Estate Brokers L.LC**.

The page must introduce the company, explain its values and approach, build trust, and present a small leadership team.

The design must remain elegant, modern, spacious, and realistic to implement.

Do not create an experimental or award-style layout.

---

## Visual Direction

The page should feel:

- Premium
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Modern
- Spacious
- Corporate
- Refined

Avoid:

- Complex interactions
- Heavy animation
- Excessive cards
- Large decorative graphics
- Over-designed layouts
- Generic corporate template styling
- Unnecessary statistics
- Long text blocks

Use the SMB brand colors:

- Navy Blue: `#0a2342`
- Golden Yellow: `#f9ba3f`

Gold must be used sparingly as an accent.

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Inner Page Hero

Purpose:

Introduce the company and establish a premium first impression.

Content:

- Eyebrow: `About SMB`
- Main heading
- Short supporting paragraph
- Large background or supporting image
- Optional breadcrumb

Suggested message direction:

SMB Real Estate Brokers helps clients buy, sell, manage, and invest in residential and commercial properties across Dubai.

The hero must be shorter than the homepage hero.

---

## 2. Company Introduction

Purpose:

Explain who SMB is.

Structure:

- Two-column layout
- Company story and introduction on one side
- Supporting image on the other side

Content should cover:

- Dubai-based real estate brokerage
- Established in 2021
- Residential and commercial property services
- Client-first approach
- Local market knowledge
- Transparent advice
- Long-term relationships

Keep the copy concise and easy to scan.

---

## 3. Mission and Vision

Purpose:

Clarify the company direction.

Structure:

Two balanced content blocks:

### Mission

Help clients make informed property decisions through honest guidance, market knowledge, and dedicated support.

### Vision

Become a trusted real estate partner for buyers, sellers, landlords, and investors in Dubai.

Do not use oversized icons.

Use typography, spacing, subtle borders, or controlled background contrast.

---

## 4. Our Values

Purpose:

Explain how the company works.

Display four values:

- Transparency
- Client Commitment
- Market Knowledge
- Professional Support

Each value must include:

- Short title
- One concise description
- Optional small icon or number

The section must not look like a software features grid.

Keep the cards light and refined.

---

## 5. Why Clients Choose SMB

Purpose:

Translate the company values into practical benefits.

Suggested points:

- Honest and clear property guidance
- Access to selected opportunities
- Support from enquiry to transaction
- Knowledge of Dubai communities and developers
- Responsive and personal communication

Use a clean split layout, numbered list, or structured text blocks.

Do not repeat the exact wording from the values section.

---

## 6. Team

Purpose:

Present the people behind the company.

Display exactly **two team members**.

Each team member card must include:

- Portrait
- Full name
- Job title
- Short professional introduction
- Optional LinkedIn or email link

Structure:

- Two equal team cards
- Large, professional portraits
- Minimal card styling
- Consistent image ratio
- Clear name and role hierarchy

Do not add placeholder team members beyond the two required profiles.

If final information is unavailable, use clearly marked temporary content that can be replaced later.

The section should feel personal and credible, not like a large corporate directory.

---

## 7. How We Work

Purpose:

Show the company approach.

Display a simple four-step process:

1. Understand
2. Advise
3. Shortlist
4. Support

Each step must include a short description.

Use a horizontal process on desktop and stacked steps on mobile.

Keep the design minimal.

---

## 8. Company Commitment

Purpose:

End the company story with a strong trust message.

Content direction:

SMB focuses on long-term client relationships, transparent communication, and property decisions aligned with each client’s goals.

Use a visually distinct section with:

- Strong heading
- Short paragraph
- Optional supporting image or restrained dark background

Do not add exaggerated claims.

---

## 9. Final CTA

Purpose:

Encourage the visitor to contact the company.

Include:

- Strong headline
- Short supporting text
- Primary contact button
- WhatsApp button

Suggested direction:

`Let’s discuss your property goals in Dubai.`

---

## 10. Footer

Reuse the same corporate footer system used across the website.

Include:

- Company information
- Quick links
- Contact information
- Social links
- Copyright

# General Rules

- Keep the page visually consistent with the homepage and project landing page.
- Reuse the same header, footer, typography, buttons, spacing, forms, and design tokens.
- Maintain generous spacing.
- Keep paragraphs narrow and readable.
- Use subtle background changes to separate sections.
- Use large imagery but avoid image-heavy layouts.
- Keep cards simple.
- Avoid excessive borders and shadows.
- Use subtle hover and reveal effects only.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure complete desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Keep the page concise.
- Do not add statistics unless verified company data is available.
- Do not invent awards, partnerships, achievements, or client numbers.
- Do not use autoplay carousels.
- Do not introduce unnecessary dependencies.

The final page must communicate:

- Who SMB is
- What the company believes
- Why clients can trust it
- How the team supports clients
- Who the two key team members are
- How to contact the company
